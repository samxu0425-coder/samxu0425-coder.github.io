PianoFi turns a song into piano sheet music. I built the backend that runs the models and trained them, and it picked up its first users within a week of launch.

PianoFi Live

After PianoFi I wanted the same transcription model to work while someone is actually playing, so I built an engine in C++ that listens to audio and draws the notes on a piano roll in your browser about a second behind. Most of the time went into getting it fast enough on a GPU and cheap enough to leave online.

Try it live ↗

Triv.AI

Triv.AI is a trivia game made from your own notes: you upload a PDF, it writes the quiz, and you play it live with friends while it grades what everyone types. I built the whole thing, with a Flask server in Python behind it.
